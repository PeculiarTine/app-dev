# ***Scorpion (TV Series)***
*“Brains solve problems. But teamwork saves lives.” — Walter O’Brien*

## ***Overview***

The Scorpion is a series of American action-drama which takes the life of the real genius Walter O’Brien as its inspiration, the man who claims to have one of the highest IQs in the world. The plot revolves around very intelligent and yet socially incompetent people who get together in order to overcome the high-tech threats posed to humanity.

## ***Main Plot***

The team of geniuses which is known as Team Scorpion is the main focus of the series. The extraordinary minds of this group are employed to deal with the global crises. Cyber-attacks, nature calamities, conspiracy of governments, and such high-stakes missions are some of the matters they deal with.
